# 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。
在 issue、PR、Discussions 和其他项目空间里，请友善、尊重不同背景和经验水平的人，就事论事。
骚扰、人身攻击、公开他人隐私（包括别人的行程和住址）不被接受。

遇到问题，请通过维护者 GitHub 主页上公开的联系方式私下告知；维护者会保密处理，并可能删除相关内容或限制参与。

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be kind and respectful to people of every background and experience level in issues, pull requests, Discussions
and other project spaces. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information (including
their itineraries and addresses) are not acceptable. Report problems privately through the contact details on the
maintainer's GitHub profile; reports are handled confidentially and may lead to content removal or a ban.
